# Myspace

## Royal Enfield Interceptor 650 — Cost Tracker

An interactive, single-file spreadsheet to plan the **total cost of ownership**
of a Royal Enfield Interceptor 650.

👉 Open **[`interceptor-650-cost-tracker.html`](interceptor-650-cost-tracker.html)**
in any browser — no build step, no dependencies, no internet required.

### What it tracks
- **On-road price** — ex-showroom, RTO, insurance, accessories, handling
- **Loan & EMI** — down payment, interest rate, tenure → monthly EMI + total interest
- **Running costs** — distance, mileage and petrol price → fuel cost & ₹/km
- **Maintenance** — add/remove recurring yearly items (service, tyres, insurance renewal…)
- **Annual breakdown** — live bar chart of EMI vs fuel vs maintenance
- **Long-term projection** — total cost of ownership and effective ₹/km over N years

### Features
- Every value is editable — live recalculation as you type
- Auto-saves to your browser (localStorage); **Reset defaults** restores the sample model
- **Export CSV** for use in Excel / Google Sheets
- **Print / PDF** for a clean shareable summary

> All figures are user-editable estimates in INR, not dealer quotes. Defaults are
> indicative mid-2020s metro figures — replace them with your own.
